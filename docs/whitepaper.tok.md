# ilo Agrinet — lipu lili (P3-002)

**Network Theory Applied Research Institute**

https://github.com/NTARI-RAND/Agrinet

---

## 1. ilo ni li seme?

ilo "Agrinet" li nasin toki pi ma ale. ona li wan e jan mute pi pali kasi: jan pali kasi, jan pana, jan kama jo, jan tawa, jan pali sin pi moku. jan ni ale li ken toki, li ken pali sama, tan kasi lili lon ma tawa moku lon tomo.

---

## 2. nasin lawa pi kepeken (licence)

ilo ni li ilo pi jan ale. sina ken kepeken e ona, ken lukin e sitelen pali ona, ken pana e ona tawa jan ante. nasin lawa ona li nasin "AGPL-3.0".

sitelen ale pi nasin lawa ni li lon toki Inli taso. ona li lon ala lipu ni. o lukin e lipu Inli `whitepaper.md`.

---

## 3. jan seme li kepeken ona?

- jan ale li ken kepeken: jan wan, kulupu esun, kulupu lawa. taso jan ale li wile kepeken e nasin lawa "AGPL-3.0".
- ilo ni li pona tawa jan pi pali ma sin (terraforming) en jan pi tomo pali ona sama (homesteading).
- jan li wile kepeken e sinpin ilo (user interface) tawa toki tawa Agrinet. sinpin pi NTARI li "Fruitful": https://ntari.org/fruitful

## 4. nasin pana toki

lipu Inli li pana e nasin sitelen pi toki ale — nimi, nanpa, en sitelen kipisi. lipu ni li sitelen ala e ona. lipu ni li toki e wile pi nasin ni taso.

### 4.1 nasin sitelen

toki ale li jo e kipisi mute; sitelen lili wan li kipisi e ona. toki li pini kepeken nimi wan ("over"). wile pi nasin ni li ni: ilo lili li ken sona e kipisi ale, li ken pali kepeken toki ale lon nasin sama, li ken sona e pini pi toki.

### 4.2 sinpin li kama sona

toki ale tawa Agrinet li open kepeken ilo open (key) pi sinpin. toki pi ilo open ala li tawa nasin pi kama sinpin sin.

sinpin sin li pana e nimi ona, e sona ona, e nimi pi jan lawa ona, e toki ona, e nimi ma (URL) ona. ona li wile pini e pali pi lukin (test) kin. ni la, sinpin li kama lon poki sona pi sinpin ale.

### 4.3 ilo open (key)

Agrinet li jo e poki pi ilo open. ona li pali e ilo open kepeken nasin len "QC-MDPC McEliece" — nasin len ni li awen pona lon tenpo pi ilo sona sin ("quantum").

- sinpin wan li jo e ilo open 7.
- ilo open ale li jo e tenpo pini ona. tenpo pini la, ilo li pana e ilo open sin, li weka e ilo open pini.
- toki ale li kepeken ilo open tu. ilo li lukin e ona tu.
- ilo open li wan ala e sinpin la, ilo li pini e nasin kama. jan li wile toki tawa jan pana pona pi kulupu.

### 4.4 jan li kama sona

kipisi kama pi toki li pana e sona pi jan: nimi ona, ma ona (nanpa pi ma), nimi lipu ona (email), en nanpa toki ona (phone). jan sin la, sinpin li kama jo e sona ni, li pana e ona tawa poki sona ona.

### 4.5 kipisi pi toki

**lipu esun.** jan li ken pana e lipu pi ijo anu pali ona. kulupu pi lipu ni li ni:

- pali (labor): pali pi sona nasin, pali pi pona ilo, pali pi kasi lili en pali pi kama jo moku
- tawa (logistics): tawa lili, tawa suli
- pali sin pi moku (processing): lon tomo, lon tomo pali, lon tomo pi soweli
- ma pona: pali pi kasi moli en pali pi kepeken sin
- pali pona pi ma (environmental services)

lipu ante li lon kin: lipu pi tawa ma kasi (agrotourism), lipu pi esun sama — jan pana li pana e ijo tawa jan kama jo, jan esun ante li lon ala (direct market), lipu pi pali kama (plan), en lipu pi ijo (product).

**wile lukin esun (market broadcast).** jan li wile la, ilo li pana tawa ona e lipu esun pi ma poka ona. ni li kepeken lipu ma, lipu tenpo, ilo pi ante toki (jan toki li lon ala; ilo sona li ante e toki), en sona pi kasi tan AGRIS, tan Agrontology, tan AGROVOC.

**esun (transaction).** jan li kama jo e ijo la, ilo li lukin e nanpa pi lipu esun. jan tu li ken ala kama jo e ijo sama wan. esun pi tenpo kama la, mani li awen lon poki (escrow) tawa tenpo pini pi pali. esun ante la, esun li pini lon tenpo sama.

**toki jan (messages).** jan tu li ken toki tan lipu esun anu tan esun ona. toki ale li wile jo e nanpa pona (LBTAS).

**sona pi pali kama (PING).** jan pali kasi li pana e sona sin lon tenpo mute: lon suno ale, anu lon tenpo esun wan, anu lon tenpo mun wan. ona li ken pana e sitelen anu sitelen tawa kin (5 taso).

**poki sona Mycelium.** toki ale li kama len, li kama lon poki awen (blockchain), lon nasin ni: nanpa pi lipu esun, nanpa pi esun, nanpa pi sona PING, nanpa pi sona sin, en toki jan. ni li pana e sona awen pi esun ale.

### 4.6 nasin LBTAS

LBTAS li nasin pi pana nanpa pona tawa esun. ona li tan pali pi jan Nancy Leveson — ona li pali e nasin pi lukin ilo sona lon waso (aircraft).

nasin pi 5 sike suno (5 stars) li ike tan ni: ona li pana ala e sona pona tawa jan pana; ona li tan esun pi tomo lape lon ma Mewika lon tenpo 1958; ona li nasin toki pi jan tu ala. nasin Leveson li jo e nanpa 6, tan +4 tawa -1. nimi ale li jo e sona mute; ni la jan li wile ala sitelen e toki mute.

| nanpa | nimi | ona li seme? |
|---|---|---|
| +4 | pona mute (Delight) | esun li sona e wile kama pi jan; ona li pona lon tenpo kama kin |
| +3 | pakala ala (No Negative Consequences) | esun li awen e jan tan pakala; ona li pona mute e wile |
| +2 | pona (Basic Satisfaction) | esun li pona tan nasin kulupu; ona li suli tan wile toki pi jan |
| +1 | pona lili (Basic Promise) | esun li pana e wile taso; ona li pana ala e ijo ante |
| 0 | pona ala pona (Cynical Satisfaction) | esun li pana e wile lili taso; jan pana li kepeken ala e wawa |
| -1 | pona ala (No Trust) | jan li pakala anu li kama jo e ijo ike tan nasin ike pi jan pana |

jan pana en jan kama jo li wile pana e nanpa ni lon toki ale pi Agrinet. jan kama jo li ken pana kin e nanpa lon tenpo mun 3 pi esun pini; jan pana li ken pana e nanpa pi jan pana nanpa lon tenpo sama.

ilo li pana ala e nanpa wan taso (average); ona li pana e sona ale pi nanpa ale. wile ni li ni: kulupu li ken lukin e nasin ike pi jan, li ken pona e ona. jan lawa li jan — ilo sona li lawa ala e ni.

nasin LBTAS: https://github.com/NTARI-RAND/Leveson-Based-Trade-Assessment-Scale
